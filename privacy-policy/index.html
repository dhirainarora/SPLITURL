Privacy Policy

Split Roulette provides a web-based bill-splitting game. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the website.

Information and browser storage

Bill amounts, participant names, settings, and recent split history are processed in your browser and may be stored in local browser storage. The site does not require an account. Hosting providers may process standard technical request logs such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time for security and diagnostics.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users can manage personalized advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies subject to their own policies. Please review the relevant provider policies for more details.

Data choices

You can clear browser storage and site data through your browser settings. For privacy questions, contact dhirainarorabusiness@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you have a privacy concern about information submitted by a child, please contact us.
